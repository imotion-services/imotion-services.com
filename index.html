Manifesto

The future of enterprise software

We believe the future of enterprise software is a world where every company operates its own Software/AI garden (or a farm or a foundry if you like it) — a trusted, on-demand capability to design, build, and evolve Software Solutions and AI agents for the business challenges of every stakeholder: colleagues, customers, partners, and communities alike.

Agentforce-powered Factories

For Salesforce — similar to other enterprise software giants — this means Agentforce-powered Factories that empower every user with personalized sales, service, production, delivery, and personal development agents. This is not simply machine learning software-empowered automation — it is democratized market and industry intelligence and amplified human potential.

Three scenarios

Three scenarios. Only one builds a business.

The edges are loud. The middle — where we look for rational cooperation — is quieter. That is where we work.

  • Edge

    Cyborg

    All in

    Technology replaces. People adapt to the machine’s pace. Fast, spectacular — and the business easily loses its own intent.

  • Middle

    Coexistence

    Human + machine, with intent

    We don’t wait out the storm, and we don’t dissolve into it. We build complementary capabilities: people decide, machines scale, the system learns.

  • Edge

    Last Jedi

    Remove the technology

    Preserve the values until “the storm passes”. A respectable reflex — but the first-mover advantage and ballast knowledge pile up in the meantime.

Most of us are here: looking for rational cooperation between people and AI — and preparing for what comes next. Not a cyborg. Not a hermit. An entrepreneur.

Approach

How we work

Four ideas behind our work with customers and partners on Salesforce, Data and AI.

  • Untapped value

    Existing Salesforce investments often hold trapped value: in data silos, unused application capabilities, AI that stays in pilots, low user adoption and industry processes that were never localized. We find where it sits and how to release it.

  • The Forward Deployed Engineer

    A Forward Deployed Salesforce Solution Engineer works embedded with the customer’s team, bridging strategy, technology and operations. The role complements consultants and account teams rather than replacing them, and drives faster cycles and adoption.

  • The Lab’s four pillars

    • Methodology – industry playbooks and implementation kits
    • Competency – training, simulation and skill validation
    • Demo & Model architecture – live demos, sandbox validation and reference solution models
    • Quality assurance – QA and RFP/RFI validation

  • Value for customers and partners

    For customers: trapped value unlocked in existing investments, faster time-to-value, higher adoption and industry-fit solutions.

    For implementation partners and third-party FDE teams: joint pilots, FDE capacity, methodology kits, competency programmes, demo assets and QA support.

Technology focus: Data + AI + Industry – Data Cloud, Agentforce and Salesforce Industry Clouds.
